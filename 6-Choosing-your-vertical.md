# Choosing Your Vertical 
*A beginner-friendly guide to picking which vertical to build*

Source: [The AI Agent Factory](agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ecosystem/choosing-your-vertical)

---

## 1. Three rules underneath everything 

1. **The launch rule.** A vertical never launches without a committed domain expert. Without one, you just have a pile of documents, not a vertical.
2. **The first-job test.** The best first Digital FTE job: repeats daily/weekly, is measurable, is already reviewed by a human supervisor today, and mistakes can be fixed.
3. **The order rule.** Build first, sell econd. You build one complete "slice" of the profession's knowledge *before* asking a buyer for a number  a buyer shown nothing won't name his own starting number.

**The big idea:** a wrong *beachhead* (your first narrow segment) costs three months and is easy to fix. A wrong *vertical* costs a year and is very hard to fix  the corpus, the builder, and the customer relationships all live inside it.

---

## 2. Step 1 — Name one body of work, not an industry

"Healthcare" is an industry (too broad). "Medical coding review" is a vertical (narrow enough).

**The test:** can you picture one person whose whole career is exactly this work? If yes — narrow enough. If your answer is "well, several kinds of people" — cut it smaller.

> **Common trap:** a narrow AI *agent* (does one job, like drafting one message type) is not the same as a *vertical* (a governed profession you can build repeatable products from). Choose the vertical first, then decide how narrow the agents inside it should be.

---

## 3. Step 2 — Screen for selling ease

Score each question 0–10, with one sentence of evidence per score. Average them.

1. Can you define success with **one main number**?
2. Does the buyer **already spend money** on this problem?
3. How clear is the path to a first deployment? (Fewer regulators = higher score)
4. Can you **legally** get the knowledge?
5. Does an expert probably exist **in your network**?
6. Can **one shared builder** serve every customer in this domain?

**Score 6/10 or higher → continue. Below 6 → take an exit:**
- **Service domain** — sells fine, but no shared corpus/expert. You still earn from it (client-specific work), just not as a full vertical.
- **Parked** — write down what would need to change to revisit it.


---

## 4. Step 3 — Run 8 tests

Score each: Pass = 1, Partial = 0.5, Fail = 0. Need **6.5 / 8** to continue.

⚠️ **Exception:** if **Test 3** fails, the evaluation ends regardless of total score — the launch rule can't be broken.

1. **Corpus shape** — does the knowledge fit into citable sources, unbendable rules, and step-by-step procedures?
2. **First Workers** — can you name 3+ Digital FTEs that pass the first-job test?
3. **Expert availability** (the deciding test) — is there a real, named person who will license their material and persona?
4. **Governing rules** — what rules govern this work, and can you name them?
5. **Market and economics** — who buys, how many, is it recurring? No invented numbers.
6. **Governance feasibility** — can the corpus have an owner, versions, and reviews within months?
7. **Competitive position** — who already serves this, and why would a buyer choose you over them?
8. **Regulatory gate** — can you design the work to stay clearly inside what regulators allow?

---

## 5. Step 4 — Choose the beachhead

Pick the segment with the clearest knowledge, the most checkable first Worker, and the shortest path to a signed contract.

**If two options look equally good — the expert decides.** Their commitment breaks the tie.

⚠️ **Rights warning:** owning a book does NOT give you the right to serve it through an AI system. Every source needs a documented rights basis (public domain, open license, commercial license, direct permission, or a replacement plan).

---

## 6. Step 5 — Validate against 4 gates in 60–90 days

**All four gates, or no launch.** Two come first, then you build one slice, then the last two become possible.

1. **The expert has signed** — not just verbally agreed.
2. **Rights basis confirmed in writing.**
3. *(Build one slice here — one professional outcome, covered completely, from the expert's real files)*
4. **A named sponsor exists** with a drafted contract of success — brought to the table BY the slice you built.
5. **Real quoted numbers**, not guesses.

---

## 7. Why this matters for your own goal

This entire method is literally how you decide what to sell and what to teach. You can't build income or a course around a vertical you picked by guessing this checklist replaces guessing with evidence.
